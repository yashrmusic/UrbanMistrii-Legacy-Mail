## Local search work

- Fully verify and complete the Google Business Profile.
- Use the real studio address and service area; do not create keyword-stuffed business names or duplicate locations.
- Set the primary category to Architect or Architecture Firm based on the options Google provides, then add Interior Designer as a secondary category.
- Add the website, project enquiry phone, current hours, services, and rights-approved project photography.
- Ask real clients for specific reviews that naturally mention the project type and city.
- Reply to every genuine review in the studio's own voice.
- Publish regular Google Business Profile updates for completed projects, press, and studio news, each pointing to the most relevant website page.

## Publishing rhythm

Each month:

- Publish one substantial project case study or genuinely useful design article on the website.
- Publish two evidence-led LinkedIn posts drawn from a real brief, drawing, site decision, or finished project.
- Pitch one rights-approved project to a respected architecture or hospitality publication.
- Update one major directory or professional profile.
- Earn one relevant local or editorial mention through real work, partnership, event, award, or publication.

## Measurement

Track outcomes in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, not by repeatedly searching from one personal device.

Primary query groups:

- restaurant architects in Delhi
- restaurant interior designers in Delhi
- hospitality design studio Delhi
- architects for restaurants in Delhi NCR
- commercial interior designers Delhi
- residential architects Delhi
- group housing architects Delhi NCR

Measure impressions, non-branded clicks, indexed project pages, referring domains, Google Business Profile calls, and qualified project enquiries. Rankings are an outcome, not the only metric.

## Guardrails

- Do not buy bulk backlinks, reviews, directory packages, or guest-post bundles.
- Do not create near-duplicate city pages without real local projects and useful content.
- Do not add invisible text, fake awards, unsupported project facts, or review schema for reviews the site does not display.
- Do not promise a number-one ranking. Search engines decide placement; the studio can only improve relevance, authority, consistency, and user experience.
