Hospitality / Delhi NCR

Restaurant architects in Delhi for spaces that work at table level.

If you are looking to hire restaurant architects in Delhi, Urban Mistrii designs dining rooms, bars, cafes, and hospitality interiors with guest flow, kitchen planning, atmosphere, and execution support built into the process.

What the service covers

Restaurant briefs get better when planning, operations, and atmosphere are solved together.

The studio works on hospitality projects from concept through site support, with a focus on how the room feels before, during, and after a meal. That means shaping circulation, adjacency, light, material direction, and the details that keep the space functional once real service begins.

For Delhi clients, the useful questions are usually very practical: how people enter, where they wait, how the kitchen and bar talk to the dining room, and whether the visual tone still feels right at 8pm on a busy night. That is the kind of problem this page is built to answer.

Featured project

Padmanabham shows how a restaurant can feel specific without becoming a theme.

One of Urban Mistrii’s most visible restaurant projects, Padmanabham in Janpath, New Delhi, translates Chettinad references, lotus symbolism, and a long linear site into a dining room that feels deliberate and memorable.

Scope

The work is about guest movement, service logic, and the feeling the room leaves behind.

Planning

Guest arrival, waiting zones, dining rhythms, and circulation are mapped before finishes are chosen, so the layout supports the way people actually use the room.

Service logic

Kitchen adjacencies, bar placement, storage, and back-of-house movement are designed together so operations do not fight the dining experience.

Atmosphere

Light, colour, acoustics, furniture, and materials are balanced so the restaurant feels memorable without sliding into costume.

Execution

Drawings, vendor coordination, site reviews, and design intent checks help the idea survive the build and still feel coherent on opening day.

Questions

What restaurant clients usually ask first.

Do you work outside Delhi?

Yes. The studio is based in Delhi and works across Delhi NCR and other project locations when the brief and execution structure make sense.

Can you help with kitchen and bar planning?

Yes. Hospitality layouts are always shaped around service logic as well as the guest-facing experience.

Do you support execution?

Yes. The studio can stay involved through drawing reviews, site decisions, vendor coordination, and design intent checks.

What kind of restaurant brief fits best?
