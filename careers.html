Careers at Urban Mistrii

Join Urban Mistrii

Design spaces. Build experiences.

Urban Mistrii is a Delhi-based multidisciplinary design practice working across architecture, interiors, hospitality, and urban development. We are looking for people who care about craft, precision, and the discipline required to make a space hold together after it is built.

Why Work With Us

Architecture

Work on realised projects ranging from residences to large-scale hospitality and urban development.

Interior Design

Develop interiors that balance materiality, lighting, and function across homes, restaurants, and workplaces.

Creative Problem Solving

Every project brings a new set of constraints. The studio values people who find clear answers within them.

Collaboration

Work closely with principals, vendors, consultants, and site teams in a studio that communicates openly.

Professional Growth

Take ownership of project stages, develop client skills, and build a portfolio of built work.

Design Excellence

The studio is interested in atmosphere, precision, and the quiet adjustments that make a space feel inevitable.

Hiring Process

01 Apply

Submit your application through the form below with your portfolio and resume.

02 Portfolio Review

Our team reviews your work, experience, and fit for the role and studio culture.

03 Assignment

A short design or communication exercise may be sent to understand your thinking process.

04 Interview

Meet the team to discuss your experience, design approach, and how you would contribute to ongoing projects.

05 Decision

We extend an offer or provide clear feedback. Selected profiles may also be retained for future opportunities.
