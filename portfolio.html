Studio

Spaces shaped around context, craft, and lived experience.

Urban Mistrii designs environments that feel resolved, human, and quietly memorable. Each project begins with the conditions of site, client, and community, then moves through planning, architecture, interiors, material systems, and detail direction as one coherent spatial story.

Our work spans architecture, interiors, hospitality, workplace, and spatial installations, with attention to atmosphere, movement, mood, and the practical realities of execution.

Services

From first idea to lived-in detail.

Interiors

Hospitality

Selected Work

A portfolio across hospitality, residential, workplace, and architecture.

Residential / Architecture

The Pool House

A 1,800 sq ft prefabricated farmhouse extension in Chhatarpur, Delhi, shaped as a retreat for a multigenerational family. The project uses light-gauge steel frame (LGSF) construction, a glass-and-metal envelope, a tapering cantilevered roof, and warm interior finishes to connect pool, garden, and family life.

Location
Chhatarpur, Delhi
Area
1,800 sq ft / 167 m²
Focus
Prefabrication, sustainability, family retreat
