Studio

About the studio

Founded by Ritika Rakhiani, Urban Mistrii is a multidisciplinary design practice working between architecture, interiors, hospitality, and group housing.

Each project starts with a close read of context: site, movement, mood, client ambition and the realities of execution. The work is built through plans, material studies, light, furniture, detail and the quiet adjustments that make a space feel inevitable.

2018 / Studio founded Delhi / Working across India Restaurants / Retail / Commercial / Group Housing
