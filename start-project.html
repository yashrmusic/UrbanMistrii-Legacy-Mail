Step 1 of 4

A strong brief usually covers:

  • What is the brief? — the kind of space, and who it is for.
  • What is fixed already? — location, site size, approvals, or stage.
  • What needs help most? — concept design, interiors, planning, or execution support.
  • What matters most? — budget, schedule, experience, or efficiency.

We reply within 2 working days. Your details are used only to respond to this enquiry — see our privacy note.

What Happens Next

Four clear steps, with no vague back-and-forth.

  1. A reply within 2 working days — by email or phone, directly from the studio.
  2. A call — to understand the brief, the site, and what success looks like.
  3. A site visit — measurements, light, access, and the realities of the plot or space.
  4. A fee proposal — scope, stages, and timelines in writing before any commitment.
