Before You Send

The strongest project enquiries usually answer a few practical questions upfront.

What is the brief?

Tell the studio whether this is a residence, hospitality venue, workplace, development layout, renovation, or mixed-use opportunity.

What is fixed already?

Share what is already decided: location, site size, timeline, operational model, approvals, or whether construction has already started.

What needs help most?

Be direct about whether the need is concept design, interiors, hospitality planning, architectural direction, or execution support.

What matters most?

Budget, schedule, guest experience, family use, atmosphere, or commercial efficiency can each pull a project in different directions. Knowing the priority helps.

What Happens Next

Once the brief is in, the studio can respond with the right first conversation instead of a vague back-and-forth.
