Privacy

How we handle your enquiry details.

This page explains, in plain terms, what happens to the information you share with Urban Mistrii Studio through this website.

What we collect

Only what the enquiry form asks for.

When you send the project enquiry form, we receive your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number (if you share it), company or household name (if you share it), and the project details you describe — type, location, stage, area, budget, timeline, and your brief.

The form is delivered to the studio's inbox through FormSubmit, a form-delivery service.

How we use it

Only to respond to your enquiry.

Your details are used to reply to your enquiry and, if the conversation continues, to discuss and plan your project. We do not sell your details, and we do not share them with third parties for marketing.

We keep enquiry details only for as long as they are needed to handle the enquiry and any project that follows from it.

Your requests

Ask us about your data.

To see, correct, or delete the details you have shared with us, email mail@urbanmistrii.com and we will act on your request.
