Group housing / Delhi NCR

Group housing architects in Delhi for liveable communities at every scale.

Urban Mistrii supports group housing and residential development briefs through site strategy, unit mix, massing, movement, shared spaces, climate response, facade direction, consultant coordination, and execution-aware planning.

A larger residential lens

Group housing cannot be solved as a stack of repeated floor plans.

The project has to connect land efficiency with daylight, privacy, circulation, parking, services, landscape, community space, construction logic, and the long-term quality of everyday life. A decision at block scale eventually reaches every balcony, corridor, lift lobby, and home.

The studio approaches the brief across those scales, beginning with the site and development framework before moving into buildings, units, shared spaces, material systems, and the details that shape lived experience.

Residential foundation

Built residential work gives the studio a grounded view of privacy, light, material, and family use.

The Hill House is presented as adjacent residential evidence rather than a completed group housing case study. Together with The Pool House and unit-scale interior work, it informs how Urban Mistrii thinks from private living patterns outward to larger residential frameworks.

Scope

Residential development decisions need to stay connected across planning, architecture, landscape, and delivery.

Feasibility and site strategy

Land shape, access, orientation, development controls, buildable area, phasing, and project goals are reviewed before a formal planning direction is fixed.

Massing and block logic

Building placement, height, spacing, view corridors, daylight, ventilation, privacy, fire access, and open-space structure are developed as one system.

Unit mix and planning

Unit types, efficiency, room proportions, kitchens, balconies, storage, shafts, and adaptability are tested against the intended resident profile and commercial brief.

Movement and shared space

Vehicular entry, pedestrian routes, drop-offs, parking, cores, lobbies, amenities, landscape, and social spaces are arranged to reduce conflict and support community use.

Climate and facade

Orientation, shading, openings, facade depth, material durability, maintenance, and energy performance are considered alongside architectural identity.

Coordination and delivery

Architecture is coordinated with structure, building services, fire, landscape, approvals, cost, phasing, and execution requirements throughout the project.

First-stage inputs

A serious group housing conversation begins with land, numbers, residents, and delivery structure.

Land and controls

Share the survey, ownership boundaries, approach roads, contours, applicable development controls, easements, existing conditions, and approval status.

Development brief

Define target saleable or leasable area, unit mix, resident segment, amenities, parking expectations, phasing, budget, and intended positioning.

Consultant structure

Clarify which developer, project manager, structural, services, landscape, cost, approvals, sustainability, and execution teams are already appointed.

Questions

What developers and landowners usually need to establish early.

Can Urban Mistrii begin with feasibility?

Yes. An early engagement can review the site, development brief, unit mix, constraints, consultant requirements, and possible planning directions before detailed design.

Can you work with an existing developer team?

Yes. The studio can collaborate with the client's project management, structure, services, landscape, approvals, cost, sales, and execution teams.

Can the scope include unit interiors?

Yes. Typical-unit planning, finish direction, show units, amenity interiors, lobbies, and other shared interior environments can be integrated where required.

Can the project be phased?

Yes. Phasing can be considered through site access, infrastructure, amenity delivery, construction sequencing, resident experience, and the commercial programme.

Where does the studio work?

Urban Mistrii is based in Delhi and can work across Delhi NCR and other Indian locations when the project scale and consultant structure are suitable.
