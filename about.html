About

A Delhi studio designing spaces with clarity, mood, and staying power.

Urban Mistrii works across architecture, interiors, hospitality, workplace, residential, and development-led projects. The studio’s language is grounded in material discipline, practical intelligence, and spaces that remain calm under daily use.

Studio View

We care as much about what a space feels like as how well it performs.

Context

Every project begins with site, climate, use, movement, and the emotional register the space needs to carry.

Craft

Materials, lighting, detailing, and built edges are resolved with the same seriousness as plans and elevations.

Clarity

The studio tries to remove noise, indecision, and excess so projects read as coherent from first encounter to daily use.

Founder

Ritika Rakhiani leads the studio across architecture, interiors, and design direction.

Urban Mistrii is shaped by a multidisciplinary point of view that moves comfortably between residential calm, hospitality energy, commercial clarity, and urban-scale thinking. The work aims to feel composed rather than over-designed, and generous rather than generic.

The studio collaborates with clients, consultants, vendors, and execution teams with an emphasis on follow-through. Ideas matter, but they are only useful when they survive the realities of site, timelines, and built detail.

Working Style

The studio stays close to concept, but never treats execution as someone else’s problem.

Concept with backbone

Ideas are built to survive planning constraints, budgets, vendor realities, and site decisions rather than collapse the moment execution begins.

Detail without drama

The aim is not visual noise. It is atmosphere, proportion, and built clarity that still feels strong months and years after handover.

Collaboration that holds

Clients, consultants, contractors, and vendors all affect the final space. The studio works to keep those decisions aligned instead of fragmented.
