Commercial interiors / Delhi NCR

Commercial interior designers in Delhi for workplaces that stay clear under pressure.

Urban Mistrii designs offices, workplaces, and client-facing commercial interiors around team movement, work patterns, privacy, services, material durability, brand character, and the realities of everyday use.

What the service covers

A workplace is an operating system before it becomes an image.

The useful starting point is how the organisation actually works: who needs to sit together, where confidential conversations happen, how visitors arrive, which teams need focus, and how the space may change as the company grows.

Urban Mistrii turns that information into planning, material, lighting, furniture, and service decisions that feel composed without making the workplace rigid. The goal is a space that works on a normal Tuesday, not only in photographs.

Scope

Commercial design works when planning, services, identity, and delivery are coordinated early.

Workplace strategy

Team size, adjacency, growth, hybrid work, focus, collaboration, and visitor patterns are translated into a practical space plan.

Arrival and client areas

Reception, waiting, meeting rooms, presentation spaces, and leadership areas are designed to communicate confidence without becoming performative.

Work settings

Open work areas, cabins, focus rooms, collaboration points, support spaces, and informal zones are balanced around actual work rather than a single visual formula.

Services and comfort

Lighting, power, data, HVAC, acoustics, fire requirements, and equipment planning are coordinated with the interior rather than treated as late-stage corrections.

Material and identity

Durable finishes, furniture, colour, graphics zones, and details give the organisation a recognisable environment without compromising maintenance.

Execution support

Drawings, schedules, vendor coordination, site reviews, and design intent checks help manage quality across a live commercial programme.

Commercial realities

The brief should make operational constraints visible before design development begins.

People and growth

Share current and projected headcount, department relationships, visitor volume, meeting patterns, leadership needs, and storage requirements.

Building conditions

Provide the lease plan, handover condition, structural grid, floor-to-floor height, services, fire strategy, landlord manual, and working-hour restrictions.

Budget and programme

State the fit-out budget, possession date, move-in deadline, procurement constraints, and whether the workplace must remain partly operational during the work.

Questions

What commercial clients usually ask first.

Do you design offices and workplaces?

Yes. Urban Mistrii works on offices, studios, workplaces, reception areas, meeting environments, and other client-facing commercial interiors.

Can you plan for future growth?

Yes. Headcount scenarios, flexible rooms, modular furniture, services capacity, and phased occupation can be considered during planning.

Can you work with our consultants?

Yes. The studio can coordinate design intent with MEP, HVAC, lighting, acoustics, fire, IT, security, and specialist consultants appointed for the project.

Can you support an occupied office?

Where the site and programme allow it, the design and execution strategy can be phased around continued business operations.

Do you work outside Delhi?

Yes. The studio is based in Delhi and can work across Delhi NCR and other Indian locations when the scope and execution structure are suitable.
