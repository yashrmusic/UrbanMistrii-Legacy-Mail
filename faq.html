Frequently Asked

The questions that usually shape the first serious conversation.

What kinds of projects fit the studio?

Urban Mistrii works across residences, villas, farmhouses, restaurants, cafes, bars, hospitality spaces, workplaces, retail environments, and development-led planning briefs.

Where does the studio work?

The studio is based in Delhi and works across Delhi NCR and project locations that suit the brief, timeline, and execution structure.

When should a client reach out?

As early as possible. Early involvement helps shape better layouts, cleaner budgets, and a more coherent project before site decisions begin to lock in.

Can the studio support execution?

Yes. Depending on scope, the studio can stay involved through finish decisions, design-intent reviews, vendor coordination, and key site-stage clarifications.

Do you take renovation and midstream projects?

Yes, especially where the design language, planning logic, or built quality need stronger coherence. The first step is understanding what is already fixed and what can still change.

What should a strong project enquiry include?

Location, project type, approximate size, current stage, timeline, budget context, and a clear note on what kind of help is needed from the studio.

How are services structured?

Engagements can be full-scope, concept-led, or advisory depending on the brief. The right structure usually becomes clear once the site, ambition, and delivery needs are understood.

Does the studio only do interiors?

No. Urban Mistrii works across architecture, interiors, hospitality, workplace, and larger development-led planning, depending on the nature of the brief.
