Sangeetha

The brief

The courtyard, brought to the table.

Inspired by Chettinad residences and the vernacular architecture of Tamil Nadu, the restaurant transforms the courtyard — historically the social heart of the home — into the central element of its dining environment.

The space unfolds through verandah-like transitions, creating a layered journey between intimate and communal areas. Skylights and lush indoor landscaping bring natural light and greenery into the interiors, so the room breathes the way a traditional courtyard house does.

Rather than replicating traditional architecture, the design distils its essential qualities of openness, connection, and hospitality into a contemporary setting. Rich timber detailing, terracotta accents, and handcrafted finishes establish a warm, tactile material palette rooted in regional traditions, and a collaboration with Hookkapani adds another cultural layer through bespoke artworks inspired by the region's stories and artistic heritage.

Verandahs, skylights, and green courts.

03 / Craft

Timber, terracotta, and art.

Handcrafted finishes and bespoke Hookkapani artworks root the restaurant in Tamil Nadu's material and artistic traditions.
