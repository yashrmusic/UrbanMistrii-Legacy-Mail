The brief

Slow living, designed in.

Unallome is a tranquil 12-room bed and breakfast in North Goa that embodies the region's 'susegad' philosophy of slow, intentional living — a stay designed to be lingered in, not passed through.

The recently refurbished property is embraced by a lush, tropical garden, with coastal-inspired, rustic interiors throughout. Its twelve generously-sized rooms have an airy and spacious layout, and each room boasts of its own balcony, offering a personal oasis to unwind and soak in the surroundings. Inside, a bookshelf wall and framed line art give the shared spaces the feel of a lived-in home rather than a hotel.

Set in Morjim village, a quick two-minute ride from Morjim Beach, the property also works as a community space. An in-house bistro anchors the food, while events — art journalling parties, drum circles, and mandala making among them — bring guests and neighbours together, so the stay stays social even at its most serene.
