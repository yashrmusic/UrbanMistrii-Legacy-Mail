Chinmin Farms

A family farmhouse renewed through additions and alterations — its ground and first floors reworked around a book-matched marble bar counter, family suites, and a pool house.

The brief

Added to, not replaced.

Chinmin Farms was a commission of additions and alterations rather than a new build: an existing family farmhouse whose ground and first floors were reworked for the family's next generation of use.

The scope covered two family bedroom suites with their dressers and wardrobes, a new kitchen, a lounge with a TV unit, and a pool house with an outhouse, alongside boundary-wall electrical work and a sunken court. Inside, the living space is anchored by a book-matched marble bar counter under a fluted timber ceiling, with a sculptural hanging mobile, mustard armchairs, and brass wall art over the dining table setting the tone.

The finished house was photographed in December 2023 — quiet, material-led rooms that keep the farmhouse's ease while giving the family a far more deliberate set of spaces to gather in.

01 / Approach

Additions and alterations.

The existing farmhouse was kept and reworked — ground and first floors replanned rather than rebuilt, so the new rooms sit naturally inside the old structure.

02 / Living core

Marble, timber, and brass.

A book-matched marble bar counter, a fluted timber ceiling, and a brass wall installation give the shared spaces their material weight.

03 / Outdoors

A pool house of its own.

The commission extended beyond the main house to a pool house and outhouse, with a sunken court tying the outdoor rooms together.
